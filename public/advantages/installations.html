For experiential spaces and collaborators

A space that listens to its visitors.

Imagine an environment where presence, quiet and shared timing reshape an abstract visual field. MORPHA offers a conceptual foundation for installations in which participants influence rather than command.

01 / Participation

Beyond buttons and triggers.

Guests need not learn a menu of actions. A gradual response to sound and silence can become the central experience.

02 / Shared attention

Silence becomes collective material.

The protocol proposes collaborative utterances shaped by average influence and overlapping silence. These are design ideas for future multi-participant implementations, not current live network features.

03 / Flexible medium

From intimate room to large projection.

The protocol does not demand a specific display, device or artistic aesthetic. The reference browser renderer is one possible expression.
