01 / Musical expression

Not another equalizer.

Instead of tying brightness or scale directly to loudness, MORPHA frames audio as pressure on motion, resistance and persistence. A quiet phrase can matter as much as a loud one.

Current implementation: microphone-driven glyph behavior and audio influence mapping. Fine-grained per-glyph influence remains a development milestone.
02 / Artistic freedom

Leave space for interpretation.

No dictionary of fixed visual meanings. The same performance can invite different readings, enabling ambiguity, improvisation and unresolved endings.

03 / Compositional vocabulary

Work with decay, not cuts.

Consider transitions, silence, interruption and overlap as compositional tools—useful for live electronics, experimental jazz, dance and audiovisual performance.
